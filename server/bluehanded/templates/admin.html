Path loss calibration

  1. Pick one node and a test phone/earbud. Find its MAC on the dashboard.
  2. Hold the device at a measured distance (start at 1 m) for 10–20 seconds, then click Record point.
  3. Repeat at 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 m (and with other nodes if you like), then click Fit model.
